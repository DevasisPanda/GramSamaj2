The journey of the Appropriate Institute of Integrated Rural Development (AIRD) began on 3 December 2019, inspired by the philosophy of the Development Car (DC), introduced by an Australian social worker committed to serving underprivileged communities in India. The concept encouraged people to view service to the needy as a means of spiritual growth and social transformation.
A series of meetings held between 4 December 2019 and 28 January 2020 laid the foundation for AIRD. These meetings resulted in the decision to establish a public charitable trust, identify Village Garhi (Malihabad, Lucknow) as the demonstration village, involve local community leaders in the governance of the organization, and formulate the vision of demonstrating Gram Swaraj through participatory development.
On 31 January 2020, AIRD was formally registered as a Public Charitable Trust. Soon afterwards, the organization initiated the conceptualization of the KRANTI (Key to Reform and Adopt Noble Treatment Initiatives) project in Village Garhi.
During March 2020, AIRD introduced computerized documentation of SHG transactions to promote transparency and organized community meetings on the relationship between spirituality and development. The organization also obtained its Permanent Account Number (PAN: AAHTA8244M), enabling it to establish its financial systems.
The year 2020–21focused on strengthening community participation despite the challenges posed by the COVID-19 pandemic.
Earth Day was celebrated on 22 April 2020, marking AIRD's first environmental awareness campaign organized with Self-Help Groups. Women and children participated in slogan writing, poster preparation, and awareness activities promoting environmental conservation.
On 15 August 2020, Independence Day was celebrated through a community rally carrying environmental and development messages. Villagers also pledged to work collectively towards establishing Gram Swaraj in Village Garhi.
The birthday of Mr. Christopher Baron ("Bank Uncle") was celebrated on 25 September 2020, reinforcing the values of compassion, mutual support, and service to the needy.
After reviewing progress in different villages, AIRD decided to concentrate its efforts exclusively on Village Garhi, where community participation was strongest. The organization's Foundation Day was celebrated on 3 December 2020, reaffirming its commitment to developing a live model of participatory rural development.
AIRD also opened its first State Bank of India account on 30 June 2020, strengthening institutional financial management.
During 2021–22, AIRD focused on institutional development and refining its strategy for implementing the KRANTI project.
Earth Day was celebrated on 22 April 2021 as a tribute to Prof. M. M. Hoda, recognized for his contributions to Appropriate Technology in India. The event promoted participatory approaches to sustainable rural development.
A special meeting with SHGs reaffirmed the importance of transparency in financial management and revived efforts to digitally document SHG activities.
On 25 September 2021, SHGs celebrated Bank Uncle Day, promoting the philosophy of happiness through service to the needy.
The Foundation Day on 3 December 2021 included meditation and reflection at Ramakrishna Math, while AIRD continued developing its official website to document village initiatives and invite collaboration from individuals and institutions.
Between 26 and 30 January 2022, AIRD organized a five-day workshop to refine the process of participatory governance. On 2 February 2022, SHG members discussed establishing an Appropriate Ashram in Village Garhi, and a Project Implementation Team (PIT) comprising active SHG leaders was formed.
On 4 March 2022, development experts visited Village Garhi to jointly conceptualize the KRANTI Project with local residents.
The Annual Meeting of the Board of Trustees, held on 30 March 2022, reviewed organizational progress and approved a roadmap for implementing KRANTI through participatory action research, capacity building of youth as Change Agents, strengthening Gram Panchayat institutions, and promoting transparency through digital information systems.
During the year, AIRD also strengthened its institutional standing by obtaining registration with NITI Aayog and recognition under Sections 12A and 80G of the Income Tax Act, enabling it to receive tax-exempt donations and expand partnerships.
Thus between 2019 and 2022, AIRD successfully established itself as a community-based rural development organization committed to demonstrating Gram Swaraj through participatory governance. Despite limited financial resources and the disruption caused by the COVID-19 pandemic, the organization laid a strong institutional foundation, built trust with the community of Village Garhi, strengthened women's Self-Help Groups, initiated digital transparency systems, and developed the KRANTI framework for creating a replicable model of sustainable, community-led rural development. The organization remains committed to transforming this vision into a practical reality through collaboration with communities, government agencies, academic institutions, and development partners.
