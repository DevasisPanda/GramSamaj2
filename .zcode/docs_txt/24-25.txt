The financial year 2024–25 commenced with a cash balance of ₹470, while the bank balance was nil. Despite limited financial resources, AIRD continued its efforts to strengthen community participation and prepare village Garhi as a live model of participatory rural development.
Annual meeting of the board of trustees
On 2 April 2024, the Board of Trustees held its annual planning meeting. After reviewing the progress made in village Garhi, the Board resolved not to discontinue the initiative. Instead, it decided to intensify community mobilization through the women's Self-Help Groups (SHGs), recognizing them as the most active institution in the village.
The Board reaffirmed its commitment to developing village Garhi as a demonstration village for community empowerment and Gram Swaraj through participatory planning and collective action.
Earth Day celebration
On 22 April 2024, AIRD celebrated Earth Day in village Garhi with members of the SHGs and their children.
Children participated enthusiastically in a placard-making competition, using environmental slogans selected during previous awareness campaigns. The participants also organized a slogan-chanting programme to spread awareness on environmental conservation. Outstanding entries were recognized with small prizes, encouraging children to become ambassadors of environmental protection in their community.
National Panchayati Raj day
On 25 April 2024, AIRD celebrated National Panchayati Raj Day in village Garhi. A representative from Sarvajani Shikshonnayan Sansthan (SSS) participated in the programme to observe AIRD's field activities and understand its participatory approach to rural development.
The interaction provided an opportunity to discuss AIRD's research on community empowerment and its vision of developing a live model village without dependence on external project funding.
Partnership with SSS
Following a series of discussions with Dr. S. C. Trivedi "Madhupesh," Head of Sarvajani Shikshonnayan Sansthan (SSS), the organization extended its support to AIRD's initiative.
On 5 May 2024, SSS contributed ₹20,000 to help AIRD continue its field activities and strengthen preparations for establishing a live model of participatory rural development in village Garhi. The Board acknowledged this timely support as an important encouragement for the organization.
Spiritual camps and community meetings
With the support of SSS, AIRD organized its first Spiritual Camp on 21 May 2024, integrating spiritual values with community development.
This was followed by a community meeting on 26 May, during which the objectives of Project KRANTI and the role of villagers in achieving Gram Swaraj were discussed in detail.
Additional community meetings held on 26 May and 29 May further strengthened dialogue with villagers. Community members expressed their willingness to participate actively in future development initiatives.
Field assessment by subject experts
On 5 June and 18 June 2024, teams of subject experts visited village Garhi to assess community preparedness for implementing Project KRANTI.
The experts interacted with SHG members, youth, and villagers to understand their expectations and willingness to participate in community-led development activities.
Experience-Sharing Workshop
On 27 June 2024, an experience-sharing workshop was organized at the AIRD office. The meeting was attended by subject experts and members of the Board of Trustees.
Based on field observations, the participants concluded that although villagers appreciated the concept of Project KRANTI, limited availability of time among community members was a major constraint affecting their regular participation.
The workshop also highlighted an important lesson: community participation becomes more sustainable when a few local individuals are formally assigned responsibilities and provided with a modest honorarium. Such locally appointed workers can maintain regular communication with villagers and continue activities initiated by external experts.
This experience led AIRD to reconsider its earlier strategy of depending entirely on voluntary participation.
Bank Uncle Day
On 25 September 2024, AIRD celebrated Bank Uncle Day at its office to honour the contribution of Mr. Christopher Baron, whose philosophy of serving the needy has continued to inspire the organization's work.
Development Car Day
On 3 December 2024, AIRD celebrated Development Car Day, commemorating the birthday of the founder of the Development Car concept.
Members reflected on the importance of integrating spiritual awareness, compassion, and selfless service with participatory rural development.
KRANTI Planning Workshop
On 27 December 2024, AIRD organized a workshop to review its field experiences and prepare an action plan for project KRANTI.
The workshop examined the findings of AIRD's participatory field research and finalized a practical framework for implementing KRANTI as an action-oriented participatory development project. The participants agreed that the project should demonstrate the process of community empowerment in practice rather than only in theory.
Development of educational material
During December 2024, AIRD developed educational materials based on its spiritual teachings and philosophy of community development.
With the support of professional designers, a multimedia slide presentation was prepared to create awareness about the purpose of human life, selfless service, community participation, and the vision of Gram Swaraj. After several rounds of discussion and refinement, the educational material was finalized for future awareness programmes and training.
Board review meeting
On 30 December 2024, the Board of Trustees reviewed the year's achievements and finalized Project KRANTI as a comprehensive action project ready for partnership with organizations sharing similar development objectives.
The Board expressed its gratitude to Dr. S. C. Trivedi "Madhupesh" and Sarvajani Shikshonnayan Sansthan (SSS) for their financial support and valuable guidance.
The Board also recognized that the field experience had demonstrated the necessity of engaging paid community facilitators alongside volunteers to ensure continuity and sustainability of village-level activities.
Project proposal development: On 5 January 2025, members of the Board worked collectively to prepare Project KRANTI as a detailed project proposal for submission to government agencies, CSR foundations, universities, NGOs, and other institutions interested in participatory rural development.
The proposal was finalized with the objective of establishing long-term partnerships for implementing the project.
Foundation Day: On 31 January 2025, AIRD celebrated its Foundation Day at its office.
During the meeting, the Board reviewed the organization's journey and reaffirmed its long-term commitment to establishing village Garhi as a demonstration model of community empowerment and Gram Swaraj.
Recognizing the organization's limited financial resources, the Board resolved that, until a suitable institutional partner is identified, AIRD would continue to celebrate important national and organizational days, maintain community relationships, and preserve the momentum of the initiative while seeking support for full-scale implementation of Project KRANTI.
The financial year 2024–25 proved to be a significant learning year for AIRD. The organization continued to strengthen its relationship with the community while refining its implementation strategy through continuous field interaction and expert consultation.
The most important lesson learned during the year was that voluntary participation alone is insufficient for sustaining intensive community development programmes. Successful implementation of a live model village requires a combination of motivated volunteers, trained local leaders, and dedicated community facilitators supported through modest honoraria.
The partnership initiated with Sarvajani Shikshonnayan Sansthan (SSS) provided both financial support and strategic guidance, enabling AIRD to validate and strengthen its approach. The completion of Project KRANTI as a comprehensive action proposal marks an important milestone in AIRD's institutional development.
Although the demonstration project has not yet been launched, the organization has established a clearer implementation strategy, developed educational and training materials, and strengthened its readiness for future collaboration. AIRD remains committed to transforming the vision of Gram Swaraj into a practical, replicable model of participatory rural development through partnerships with institutions sharing similar goals.
