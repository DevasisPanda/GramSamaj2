The financial year 2023–24 commenced with a cash balance of ₹2,570 and a bank balance of ₹4,026.07.
Annual meeting of the board of trustees
On 2 April 2023, the Board of Trustees held its annual review meeting to assess the progress made during the previous financial year.
The Board appreciated the conceptual framework of Project KRANTI (Key to Reform and Adopt Noble Treatment Initiatives) and reaffirmed its vision of developing Village Garhi as a live model of participatory rural development and Gram Swaraj. However, after reviewing the field situation, the Board concluded that the level of community preparedness and participation was still below expectations.
The members emphasized that successful implementation of KRANTI would require stronger community ownership, greater participation of Self-Help Groups (SHGs), village youth, and the Gram Panchayat, and sustained confidence-building efforts. Accordingly, the Board resolved to intensify community mobilization before formally launching the project.
Earth day celebration
On 22 April 2023, AIRD celebrated Earth Day at its office. The occasion also marked the birth anniversary of Prof. M. M. Hoda, widely regarded as the pioneer of Appropriate Technology in India.
Members paid tribute to his outstanding contribution to rural development and reflected on his inspiring message:
"The past is history, the future is uncertain, and the present is a gift from the Almighty. Therefore, act today."
The meeting reaffirmed AIRD's commitment to translating ideas into practical action for sustainable rural development.
National Panchayati Raj Day
On 25 April 2023, a team of AIRD members visited Village Garhi to celebrate National Panchayati Raj Day with members of the Self-Help Groups and the Project Implementation Team (PIT) of KRANTI.
Discussions focused on strengthening people's participation in Gram Sabha, improving the functioning of village institutions, and preparing an action plan for community-led development. The villagers welcomed AIRD's continued support and agreed to work towards preparing a participatory action plan for the village.
Bank Uncle Day
On 25 September 2023, AIRD celebrated Bank Uncle Day in honour of Mr. Christopher Baron, whose encouragement for serving the needy has inspired AIRD's work.
The day began with a special prayer at Swami Ramakrishna Math, followed by a meeting at the AIRD office where members reaffirmed their commitment to selfless service and participatory rural development.
Regulatory compliance
During the year, AIRD had planned to mobilize resources for implementing Project KRANTI. However, the organization faced a significant administrative challenge.
On 1 December 2023, the Income Tax Department rejected AIRD's application relating to registration under Sections 12AB and 80G of the Income Tax Act. The application could not be approved because audited financial statements were not available for the relevant period, as the organization had undertaken only limited financial transactions and no statutory audit had been conducted.
The Board resolved to complete all necessary statutory compliances and reapply after fulfilling the required legal and financial conditions.
Development Car Day
On 3 December 2023, AIRD celebrated Development Car Day, commemorating the birthday of the founder of the Development Car concept.
Members reflected on the philosophy of combining spiritual values with social responsibility and renewed their commitment to serving disadvantaged communities through participatory development.
Review meeting
On 2 February 2024, the Board of Trustees reviewed the progress of AIRD's activities and appreciated the development of the organization's website, which documents AIRD's vision, activities, and future plans.
After careful deliberation, the Board concluded that although the conceptual framework and preparatory work were satisfactory, successful implementation of Project KRANTI would require collaboration with a committed institutional partner capable of providing technical and financial support.
The Board therefore resolved to temporarily defer large-scale field implementation until a suitable partner organization joins AIRD in establishing the first live model village.
Foundation Day
On 31 January 2024, AIRD celebrated its Foundation Day, marking the anniversary of its registration as a Public Charitable Trust on 31 January 2020.
Members of the Board of Trustees reviewed the organization's journey and reaffirmed AIRD's long-term vision of demonstrating an effective model of community empowerment, participatory governance, and Gram Swaraj through Village Garhi.
The financial year 2023–24 was primarily devoted to organizational review, statutory compliance, strategic planning, and continued community engagement rather than large-scale field implementation. While progress in Village Garhi remained slower than anticipated, the Board recognized that sustainable rural development depends on building trust, strengthening community institutions, and ensuring full community participation before initiating intensive interventions.
The year also highlighted the importance of maintaining statutory compliance, including timely audits and regulatory approvals, for resource mobilization. The Board reaffirmed its belief that AIRD's strategy of establishing a demonstration village remains relevant and committed itself to pursuing partnerships with government agencies, universities, NGOs, corporate organizations, and development institutions.
Although implementation was temporarily deferred, the Board viewed the year as one of institutional consolidation and strategic preparation, laying the foundation for future implementation of Project KRANTI when adequate technical, financial, and institutional support becomes available.
