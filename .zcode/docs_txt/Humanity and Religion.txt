Humanity and Religion
Human beings have, since ancient times, sought to understand the mystery of life, the purpose of existence, and the relationship between the visible and the invisible. Every civilization and every religion has attempted to explain birth, death, and the nature of the soul. Although their explanations differ, they all encourage human beings to live a life of truth, compassion, and service.
According to AIRD's spiritual philosophy, a human being is a combination of a visible body and an invisible soul. The body is born through one's parents and provides a physical identity, while the soul is regarded as a gift of the Supreme Power that gives life to the body. The body has a name and identity, but when the soul departs, the body becomes lifeless.
The arrival of the soul into the visible world is called birth, and its departure from the body is called death. Many religious and philosophical traditions believe that while the body is mortal, the soul is immortal and continues its journey beyond physical existence.
Every individual inherits a family, a culture, and generally the religion of his or her ancestors. Religions provide guidance about the relationship between humanity, nature, and the Divine, and they teach moral responsibilities that help sustain individuals, society, and the natural world.
People define success differently. Some measure success through wealth, power, or status, while others find it in wisdom, peace, compassion, and service to humanity.
