Membership Categories and Subscription
The Appropriate Institute of Rural Development (AIRD) welcomes individuals and institutions who believe in the values of humanity, participatory development, Gram Swaraj, and sustainable rural transformation. Membership is open to students, researchers, professionals, volunteers, social workers, institutions, and all individuals willing to contribute to community development.
