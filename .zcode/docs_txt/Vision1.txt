Vision
AIRD envisions a rural development model in which every villager becomes an active participant rather than a passive beneficiary. The organization believes that sustainable development is possible only when communities themselves participate in: identifying local issues, planning development activities, implementing projects, managing local resources, monitoring progress, conducting social audits and ensuring transparency and accountability. 
Through KRANTI, AIRD seeks to demonstrate that Gram Swaraj is not merely a philosophy but a practical system of participatory governance that can transform rural communities when supported by informed citizens, empowered local institutions, and collaborative partnerships.
The concept of AIRD is founded on the belief that people are the most valuable resource for development. Government programmes, financial resources, and technologies can produce lasting results only when local communities are empowered to participate actively in their own development.
The objective is to enable individuals, institutions, researchers, students, donors, and development practitioners to observe the progress of the initiative, learn from its experiences, and participate in the collective effort of building a practical model of Gram Swaraj based on participation, transparency, accountability, and sustainable development.
Through this approach, AIRD aims to demonstrate that meaningful rural transformation is possible when communities are empowered to become active partners in planning, implementing, monitoring, and evaluating their own development.
By establishing a live demonstration village, AIRD aims to create a replicable model of participatory rural development that integrates good governance, social accountability, digital transparency, women's empowerment, youth leadership, and collective action. The ultimate goal is to contribute towards realizing Mahatma Gandhi's vision of Gram Swaraj—a village where development is planned, implemented, monitored, and owned by the people themselves.
