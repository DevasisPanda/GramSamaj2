Benefits of Membership
Not only executive but general members will have the opportunity to become active partners in demonstrating and promoting participatory rural development. Membership offers the following benefits:
Participate in Village Development Meetings: Join community meetings and Maitri Bhoj (community meals) on every full moon night (Pooranmasi) and Amawsya every month to discuss local issues, strengthen social harmony, improve public service delivery, and support sustainable management of village resources. 
Meaningful Interaction with Rural Communities: Develop one-to-one relationships with villagers to understand their aspirations, exchange knowledge, and identify opportunities for mutual learning and collaborative action. 
Experience Rural Life: Members who wish to stay in the village will be welcomed as guests by local families, providing an opportunity to experience traditional rural hospitality, food, culture, and community life. 
Work with Community Institutions: Collaborate directly with Self-Help Groups (SHGs), Gram Panchayats, youth groups, farmers' groups, and other village institutions to strengthen local governance and participatory development. 
Research and Field Learning: Undertake village studies, impact assessments, Participatory Action Research (PAR), and social audits to understand rural development challenges and identify practical solutions. 
Practical Training in Rural Development: Gain hands-on experience in participatory planning, implementation, monitoring, evaluation, and social accountability processes at the grassroots level. 
Contribute Professional Expertise: Professionals, researchers, students, and volunteers can apply their knowledge and skills in agriculture, livelihoods, education, health, environment, governance, digital documentation, and other development sectors. 
Networking Opportunities: Interact with government departments, universities, NGOs, corporate organisations, financial institutions, and community-based organisations working for rural development. 
Certificate and Recognition: Active members and volunteers contributing significantly to AIRD's programmes may receive certificates of participation, appreciation, or recognition based on their contribution. 
Support for Research and Innovation: Members will have opportunities to test, document, and demonstrate innovative approaches to participatory rural development in a live village setting. 
Contribute to the Vision of Gram Swaraj: Become part of a collective effort to strengthen democratic village institutions and help develop a model village that demonstrates transparency, accountability, community participation, and sustainable development. 
Our Motto is 
"Join Hands – Learn Together – Serve Together – Build Gram Swaraj."
